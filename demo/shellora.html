The Shellora Process

Every Shell Has
a Story to Tell

We partner with artisan communities in Sri Lanka and the Philippines, using coconut shells that would otherwise be discarded. No two pieces are identical — and that's intentional.

  1. 1

    Sustainable Harvest

    Shells are sourced from coconut farms — a natural by-product, never cut for shell production alone.

  2. 2

    Hand Selection

    Master artisans select shells by grain, density, and curvature. Only the finest 15% pass our standards.

  3. 3

    Carving & Polishing

    Each piece is hand-carved, sanded through 6 grit stages, and finished with natural, food-safe oils.

Our Commitment

Luxury That Gives Back

Shellora exists at the intersection of beauty and responsibility. Every purchase makes an impact.

Zero Waste Philosophy

Every scrap of shell is repurposed. Coconut dust becomes fertiliser. Nothing leaves our workshop as waste.

Fair Trade Committed

Artisans earn 3× the local average wage. We invest in their community infrastructure, healthcare, and education.

Carbon-Neutral Shipping

Every shipment is offset through certified reforestation projects. We plant 1 tree per order, guaranteed.
